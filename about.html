Our Story

We Clean Spaces, You Focus on Life

SparkNest began in 2011 with three cleaners and one van in Al Barsha. Today, we're a team of 230+ trained professionals serving homes, villas and offices across all seven Emirates.

We believe a clean space is the foundation of a healthy, productive life. That's why we invest in our people, use eco-certified products, and stand behind every clean with our satisfaction guarantee.

  • Our Mission

    Deliver reliable, high-quality cleaning that gives families and businesses time back.

  • Our Vision

    To be the most trusted and sustainable cleaning brand in the GCC.
